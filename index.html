03 Projects

01 UD Hackathon · 1st Place Spring 2026

Smooth-Cruize

Detects potholes in dashcam footage and turns them into a dashboard for review and work orders.

  • YOLO-based video processing pipeline in Python (92% accuracy) that detects potholes, extracts clips, and uploads detections to the dashboard.
  • Backend service integrating Supabase (Postgres, Storage, Auth) and Google Gemini to generate severity scores (1–10) and AI-based damage descriptions.
  • Next.js frontend with Google Maps visualization and a dashboard for review and work orders.
  • Kept building after the hackathon and presented it to 10 senior members at Delmarva Power.
  • FastAPI
  • Next.js
  • TypeScript
  • Supabase
  • YOLOv8

A live event photo-matching platform, deployed on Vercel, that lets attendees instantly find the photos they appear in without browsing huge galleries.
